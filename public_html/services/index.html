What we offer

01

Podcast clipping and long-form repurposing

Send us full episodes, interviews, webinars, or YouTube videos. We find self-contained moments with a clear point and cut them into short-form clips that make sense to someone who never saw the original.

02

Short-form video editing

Already recording vertical content? We handle the edit: pacing, cuts, B-roll, sound, colour, and framing, so each video is ready to publish.

03

Hooks and content packaging

The first seconds decide whether anyone stays. We shape openings, on-screen titles, and structure so each video makes its point quickly and clearly.

04

Captions and motion graphics

Animated captions that stay readable on mute, plus graphic cards, kinetic type, and visual elements designed around your brand.

05

Production workflow

A clear, agreed way to hand over footage, review drafts, request changes, and receive finished files, so production doesn't depend on you chasing anyone.

06

Organic content management

Where it's part of the agreed service, we can also help organize and manage your organic publishing, so more of the process is off your plate.

What we commit to, and what we won't promise.

Frequently asked questions

What services does Creator Saga provide?

Short-form video editing, repurposing long-form recordings into clips, hooks and content packaging, captions and motion graphics, and a repeatable production and review workflow. Publishing support and organic content management can be included where they're part of the agreed scope.

Do I need to record content already?

Existing footage helps the most: podcast episodes, long-form videos, webinars, talks, or raw short-form recordings. If you're starting from scratch, tell us on the call. We'll be clear about what planning support we can offer for your situation.

Which platforms can you support?

We edit vertical short-form video for platforms such as Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and TikTok. The exact platforms, formats, and specs are confirmed as part of the scope we agree.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing depends on scope: how many videos, how much source material, and how much support you need beyond editing. We discuss this on the strategy call so any proposal fits what you actually need.

Can you guarantee viral results?

No. Performance depends on many things outside editing, including your topic, audience, timing, and the platforms themselves. What we commit to is the quality of the production work and a dependable workflow you can build consistency on.

Not sure which of these you need?

That's what the call is for. We'll look at your content and goals and suggest a scope that fits.
