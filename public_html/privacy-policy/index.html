Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information Creator Saga ("we", "us") collects through thecreatorsaga.com, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Booking a call. When you book a content strategy call, our scheduling or booking-form provider collects your name, email address, the time you choose, and any answers you give to its questions (for example, the type of content you create or your publishing frequency).
  • Inquiry form. If you send us details through our contact form, we receive your name, email address, any website or channel link you share, your answers to the multiple-choice questions, and your message.
  • Email and calls. Anything you choose to share with us directly.

Information collected automatically

  • Campaign information. If you arrive through a link containing campaign parameters (such as utm_source or utm_campaign), we keep those values for your browsing session and attach them to a booking or inquiry, so we know which campaigns lead to conversations. They do not identify you by themselves.
  • Analytics and advertising cookies, only with your consent. If you accept cookies, we use Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel to measure page visits, video plays, button clicks, and completed bookings. These services may set cookies and receive your IP address and device information. We do not send your name, email address, or message content to them. If you decline, these tools are not loaded.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider records standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested, for security and reliability.

We also load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads, and our scheduler (when used) is provided by Calendly, which sets its own cookies needed to make bookings work.

How we use information

  • To schedule and hold calls, reply to inquiries, and prepare proposals you've asked for.
  • To understand which pages, videos, and campaigns help visitors decide whether to talk to us, and to improve the site.
  • To measure the effectiveness of our advertising, if you have consented to those cookies.
  • To keep records of our conversations and client relationships.

We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run this website and our sales process, which may include our web host, Calendly or Typeform (booking), Google (analytics and fonts), Meta (advertising measurement), and our email provider. They process information on our behalf and under their own privacy terms. Some of them may store data outside your country.

How long we keep information

We keep booking and inquiry information for as long as needed to respond to you and manage any working relationship, and then for a reasonable period for record-keeping, unless you ask us to delete it sooner and we are not required to keep it.

Your choices and rights

  • You can change your cookie choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link in the site footer.
  • You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local data protection law, including the right to complain to a data protection authority.

Contact

To make a privacy request or ask a question about this policy, reach us through our contact page and mention "privacy" in your message.

Changes

We may update this policy as our website and services change. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
